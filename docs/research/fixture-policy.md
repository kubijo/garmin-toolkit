# Fixture policy

## Publication boundary

Committed FIT fixtures are synthetic and project-owned. Garmin samples, private files/traces/responses, and uncertain
material stay in ignored `.tmp/private-fixtures` and out of all output.

Third-party fixtures need an exact source/revision, compatible terms, source hash, and notices. Regenerate device cases
synthetically; never redact and commit the original.

## Shared fixture record

Each shared fixture or generated corpus lives beneath `infra/fixtures/<domain>/<case>/` and contains a `fixture.toml`
recording:

- stable identifier and purpose;
- synthetic or approved origin, generator, and revision;
- license and required notices;
- SHA-256 hashes of any committed inputs;
- hand-authored expectations;
- publication approval.

Generated bytes need a deterministic committed recipe. Cross-crate FIT tests use the feature-gated generator owned by
`garmin-fit`; `garmin-fixtures` adds fake identities and seeds storage through the production importer. Generated FIT
bytes and databases are outputs, not committed inputs.

Small hand-authored vectors used by one target stay beside that owner under `tests/fixtures`. They must remain visibly
synthetic and document any non-project origin, but do not need a shared-corpus record.

Target demo artifacts alone link `garmin-fixtures`. They use distinct application or storage roots; production artifacts
start empty or continue existing user data.

## Review

Inspect raw and decoded data for names, identifiers, serials, secrets, real times, and locations. Synthetic times and
positions must be visibly fictional; inspect unknown/developer fields too.

Review rejects missing metadata, hash drift, unapproved fixtures, and forbidden origins. Repository automation checks
file shape and secrets, but does not replace or claim to perform this semantic review.
