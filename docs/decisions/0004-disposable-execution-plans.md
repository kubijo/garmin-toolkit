# 0004: Disposable plans

## Decision

Work through numbered milestones in order. Each owns an outcome, questions, durable destinations, and deletion gate.
Completion promotes lasting facts and deletes the plan; Git keeps its history.

## Why

Permanent plans mix wishes, decisions, and completed work; archives still look current.

## Consequences

Persistent docs describe what exists. Later plans may change when earlier evidence changes their assumptions. See the
[execution queue](../plans/README.md).
